## Ingredients

- 1½ tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 600 g chicken thigh fillets, trimmed, cut into 4 cm pieces
- 1½ tbsp vegetable oil
- Firm stir-fry vegetables, e.g. carrots, broccoli, baby corn
- 400 ml can coconut milk
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 3 tsp brown sugar
- 2 large dried chillies, roughly chopped
- 250 g baby green beans, trimmed, sliced diagonally
- Steamed jasmine rice, to serve
- Fresh Thai basil leaves, to serve

## Method

1. Put the curry paste and chicken in a ceramic bowl and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for
   1 hour, if time permits.
2. Heat the oil in a wok and stir-fry the vegetables until just tender but still crisp. Remove
   them from the wok and set aside.
3. Over medium heat, stir-fry the chicken for 2–3 minutes, until lightly browned. Add the coconut
   milk, reduce the heat to low and cook, stirring occasionally, for 5–6 minutes, until the
   chicken is cooked through.
4. Stir in the fish sauce and sugar and cook for 1 minute. Add the chilli, the beans and the
   stir-fried vegetables. Cook for 3–4 minutes, until the beans are tender.
5. Spoon rice into bowls, spoon over the curry and top with basil.

## Notes

- **If the sauce looks split, that's what you want.** The oil separates out of the coconut milk
  as it cooks. It won't happen with brands that contain an emulsifier.
- **Choosing a curry paste:** pick one with chilli as the main ingredient. Pastes made with
  fresh chillies are hotter than ones made with dried. An authentic paste contains shrimp paste,
  which can mean a shorter shelf life.
- Leftover curry paste keeps in the fridge. Use it in Thai fish cakes, corn fritters, a penang
  curry or pumpkin soup.
