## Ingredients

**Satay seasoning**
- 1½ tsp ground coriander
- 1½ tsp cumin powder
- 1½ tsp turmeric
- 1½ tsp paprika (sweet or normal, not smoked or spicy)
- 1¼ tsp chilli powder, adjust to taste (not US-style "chili powder" blend)
- 3½ tsp curry powder (mild, any brand)
- 1¼ tsp cooking/kosher salt (or 1 tsp table salt)
- 2 tsp white sugar

**Chicken**
- 750g / 1.5 lb chicken thigh fillets, cut into bite-size pieces
- ½ onion (brown, white, or yellow), grated
- 2 tbsp oil, divided

**Satay sauce**
- 3–6 bird's eye chillies (or other small hot red chillies), finely chopped
- ¼ cup onion (brown, white, or yellow), finely diced (~¼ onion)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup chicken broth/stock
- ¾ cup roasted unsalted peanuts, chopped, divided
- 2 tsp kecap manis
- 3 tsp dark soy sauce
- 400g / 14oz coconut milk (preferably full-fat)
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp lime juice, to taste
- 3 makrut lime leaves (or 1 lemongrass stalk, white part only, smashed to burst open)

**To serve**
- Steamed rice (jasmine, white, or brown)
- Peanuts, chopped
- Cilantro/coriander leaves
- Fresh chilli, finely chopped

## Method

1. Combine the satay seasoning ingredients in a small bowl.

**Marinate & cook the chicken**

2. Combine the chicken with 3½ tbsp of the satay seasoning and the grated onion. Marinate for at
   least 20 minutes, ideally a few hours or overnight.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick skillet over high heat. Cook the chicken in 2 batches until
   browned all over but still raw inside. Transfer to a bowl, cover, and keep warm.

**Make the satay sauce**

4. In the same skillet, turn heat down to medium and heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Add the
   chilli, diced onion, and garlic. Sauté until the onion is translucent, about 2 minutes.
5. Add the remaining satay seasoning and cook for 1 minute.
6. Transfer to a blender with the chicken stock and ½ cup of the peanuts (reserve the rest).
   Purée until mostly smooth — some peanut chunks can remain. Pour the mixture back into the
   skillet.
7. Add the remaining ¼ cup peanuts, kecap manis, dark soy sauce, coconut milk, and peanut
   butter. Stir to combine.
8. Crush the lime leaves a bit in your hand to release their flavor, then add the lime leaves
   (or lemongrass) and the chicken to the sauce.
9. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low and simmer 15 minutes until thickened.
10. Stir in lime juice to taste. Serve over rice, garnished with peanuts, cilantro, and extra
    chilli if desired.

## Notes

- Satay sauce can be swapped with a supermarket jar sauce, but must taste and season it —
  jarred versions vary a lot in salt, sweetness, and heat, so adjust before serving.
- Marinating the chicken longer (a few hours to overnight) gives noticeably better flavor than
  the 20-minute minimum.
- Bird's eye chilli count and chilli powder amount can be scaled up or down to taste for spice
  level.
