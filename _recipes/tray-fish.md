## Ingredients

**Herb oil**
- 2 cloves garlic
- ½ bunch dill
- ½ bunch coriander
- ½ bunch parsley
- 2 chillies
- ½ large tomato (or 1 small tomato)
- Olive oil, enough to make a good paste

**Fish**
- 1 sea bream, cleaned and scaled
- ½ bunch dill
- Lemon slices

## Method

1. Put all the herb oil ingredients into a food processor and blend until it forms a thick,
   uniform paste.
2. Stuff the cavity of the fish with the ½ bunch of dill and the lemon slices.
3. Score the fish and spread the herb oil over both sides.
4. Put the fish in the oven at 200°C (180°C fan) for 25 minutes.
