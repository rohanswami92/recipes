## Ingredients

**Fish**
- 4 mackerel fillets (use 8 — 2 per wrap — see Notes)
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil, for searing

**Sauce**
- ¼ cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses, plus 2 tbsp more for drizzling/brushing
- 1½ tsp sumac
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp dried mint
- ½ tsp kosher salt

**Salad / fixings**
- 2 cups lettuce, chopped
- 1 white onion, thinly sliced
- ½ red bell pepper, thinly sliced
- ½ yellow bell pepper, thinly sliced

**Assembly**
- 4 pieces lavash bread

## Method

**Fish**

1. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Season the fish fillets with salt and
   pepper on both sides.
2. Once the oil is hot, sear the fish for 5 minutes on each side until fully cooked and seared.

**Sauce & fixings**

3. In a bowl, mix the olive oil, 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses, sumac, paprika, oregano, dried
   mint, and salt.
4. Toss the lettuce, onion, and peppers with 2 tablespoons of the sauce. Set aside.

**Assemble**

5. Lay a piece of lavash on a plate or board. Top with the seared fish, the lettuce/onion/pepper
   salad, and a drizzle of pomegranate molasses.
6. Roll tightly and brush the outside with more of the sauce.
7. Heat a clean pan over medium-high heat. Sear the wraps for 2–3 minutes per side until golden
   and a bit crispy.

## Notes

- Use 2 mackerel fillets per wrap for the right meat to filling and wrap ratio (double the fish
  from the original recipe if making 4 wraps).
- The sauce can also be used as a salad dressing with bitter leaves.
