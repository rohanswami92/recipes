## Ingredients

- 1.75 lb (800 g) meaty pork ribs, separated into individual ribs
- 6 cups hot water (plus more for parboiling the ribs and cooking the pasta)
- 1½ tsp salt
- 1-inch piece ginger
- 9–10 oz (250–280 g) carrots, peeled and divided (about 2 medium–large)
- 9–10 oz (250–280 g) daikon, peeled and divided
- 6–8 oz (170–225 g) short pasta of your choice

**To serve**
- Spring onions, thinly sliced
- Coriander, thinly sliced
- Black pepper
- Fish sauce, on the side
- Lime wedges, on the side
- Bird's eye chilli, thinly sliced (optional, on the side)

## Method

1. Parboil the ribs in plenty of boiling water for about a minute, then rinse and discard the
   water.
2. Put the ribs in a clean pot with the 6 cups of water, the ginger and the salt. Bring to a boil,
   lower to a simmer and skim off any foam.
3. Cut half the carrots and half the daikon into large chunks (about 3 inches) and add to the
   broth. Keep at a gentle simmer, partly covered, for 60–75 minutes until the ribs are almost
   tender.
4. Cut the remaining carrot and daikon into thin slices or small pieces, add to the pot and simmer
   about 10 more minutes until tender.
5. Meanwhile, boil the pasta in salted water, pulling it 1–2 minutes before the package time.
6. Drain the pasta. Remove the large carrot and daikon chunks from step 3, taste the broth and
   adjust the salt, then add the pasta and bring back to a rolling simmer.
7. Divide the ribs, pasta and vegetables between bowls. Top with spring onions, coriander and
   black pepper, and serve with fish sauce, lime and chilli on the side.

## Notes

- Parboiling removes impurities so the broth stays clear and clean-smelling.
- Keep the broth at a very gentle simmer and skim as needed.
- The first batch of carrot and daikon is just for sweetening the broth — it goes soft and bland,
  so discard it. The second batch is what you eat.
- Optional flavour boost: add 4–5 dried shrimp and a couple of coriander roots in step 2.
- The author prefers fusilli, but elbow macaroni works too.
