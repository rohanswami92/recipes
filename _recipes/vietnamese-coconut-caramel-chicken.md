## Ingredients

- 4 large (or 5 small) chicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
- ½ cup / 80 g brown sugar, loosely packed
- 1 tbsp water
- 400 g / 14 oz can coconut milk, low fat
- 1½ tbsp fish sauce
- 2½ tbsp rice vinegar (or cider vinegar)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 eschallot / French onion, finely sliced
- ¼ tsp white pepper (or black)
- 1 shallot (green onion/scallion), finely sliced, to garnish
- 1 large red chilli, finely sliced, to garnish

## Method

1. Place the sugar and water in a skillet over medium heat. Stir, then once it bubbles and the
   sugar is melted (it looks like caramel), add the rest of the ingredients except the chicken.
2. Stir, then put the chicken in skin side down.
3. Adjust the heat so it's simmering energetically — not rapidly, not a slow simmer.
4. After 25 minutes, turn the chicken.
5. After another 25 minutes, the fat should separate from the sauce and turn a pale brown.
6. Turn the chicken so the skin side is down again. Move it around so the sauce doesn't catch on
   the bottom of the skillet, and to brown the skin.
7. Once the sauce and skin are browned, remove from heat.
8. Place the chicken on serving plates and spoon over some sauce (chicken is already infused
   with flavor and the sauce is strong, so you don't need much).
9. Garnish with shallots and chilli, if using. Serve with rice.
