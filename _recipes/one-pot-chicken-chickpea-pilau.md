## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 chicken thighs, skin removed and trimmed of fat
- Garlic powder, onion powder, dried thyme (for seasoning the chicken)
- 2 large leeks, thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 (400g) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Grated zest of 1 lemon
- 200g easy-cook brown rice
- ~675ml chicken stock (1.5x the original 450ml)
- 1 head broccoli, broken into florets

## Method

1. Season the chicken thighs with garlic powder, onion powder, and thyme. Heat the oil in a
   large, lidded frying pan or flameproof casserole and fry the chicken for 2 mins, turning
   halfway, until lightly coloured. Lift onto a plate.
2. Add the leeks to the pan and stir-fry for 3 mins, then add the garlic and stir in the
   chickpeas, most of the lemon zest, and the rice until well mixed.
3. Pour in the stock (1.5x the usual amount) and season lightly. Cover and cook on a low heat
   until the rice is about 90% cooked, before adding anything else in.
4. Sit the broccoli florets on top of the rice, cover, and cook until the broccoli is mostly
   cooked.
5. Nestle the chicken back into the pan. Cover and continue cooking until the chicken is cooked
   through and the rice is tender, adding a splash more stock or water if it looks dry.
6. Sprinkle with the remaining lemon zest to serve.

## Notes

- Frying the chicken first — seasoned with garlic powder, onion powder, and thyme — and adding
  it back in later (rather than nestling it in raw with the rice from the start) keeps it from
  overcooking.
- Cooking the rice in extra stock (1.5x) until it's about 90% done before adding the broccoli
  and chicken back in prevents the broccoli and chicken from overcooking while the rice catches
  up.
- Original method nestles the raw chicken directly into the rice mix and cooks everything
  together, adding the broccoli on top only for the last stretch.
