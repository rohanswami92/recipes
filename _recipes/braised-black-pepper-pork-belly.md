## Ingredients

- 2 lb (900 g) pork belly
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ¼ cup fish sauce
- 1 cup water
- 3 Thai chillies
- 1 tbsp black pepper

**Garnishes**
- Spring onion
- Thai chilli
- Black pepper

## Method

1. Cut the pork belly into bite-size pieces.
2. Make the caramel (nước màu): add the sugar to a pot over medium-low heat and stir constantly
   until it turns a dark amber colour.
3. Brown the pork belly in the caramel, then add the garlic and season with the fish sauce, Thai
   chillies and black pepper.
4. Add the water, bring back up to a boil, then cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
5. Add more black pepper and adjust the seasoning to taste.
6. Serve over rice, garnished with spring onions, Thai chilli and black pepper.

## Notes

- Freeze the pork belly for about 30 minutes first to firm it up — it's much easier to cut.
- Making the caramel with sugar alone (no water or oil), stirring constantly over medium-low, is
  more reliable — the other way tends to crystallise.
- The caramel hardens when the pork goes in but dissolves again as it cooks.
- Coco Rico (coconut soda) can replace the water, though the author didn't find it made a big
  difference.
