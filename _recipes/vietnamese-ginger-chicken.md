## Ingredients

**Chicken**
- 3 lb (1.4 kg) chicken
- 1 tbsp chicken bouillon powder
- 2 tbsp granulated cane sugar
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper

**Aromatics**
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced into thin strips (about ¼ cup)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp minced shallots

**Caramel**
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar

**Braising liquid and garnishes**
- 1 cup coconut soda (or water)
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp thinly sliced coriander or spring onions
- Red chillies (optional)

## Method

1. Cut the chicken into roughly 1.5-inch chunks with a sharp cleaver. Marinate with the bouillon
   powder, sugar, fish sauce and black pepper for at least 30 minutes, or overnight for better
   flavour.
2. In a lidded skillet at least 10 inches wide, add the oil and sugar and shake gently to spread
   the sugar out. Heat over medium-high until it turns dark amber/brown — don't let it burn or it
   will taste bitter.
3. As soon as the caramel is dark brown (not black), add the ginger, garlic and shallots and fry
   about 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the chicken and toss until it takes on a light brown tint.
4. Add the coconut soda, cover, and reduce to a low simmer for 10 minutes, tossing halfway.
   Uncover and cook another 10 minutes until there's just some liquid left. Adjust seasoning to
   taste (the author adds about ½ tbsp each of fish sauce and bouillon powder here).
5. Top with black pepper, coriander or spring onions, and chillies if using. Serve with steamed
   rice and a vegetable side.

## Notes

- Cut the chicken while it's half-frozen so the bones cut cleanly instead of splintering.
- Watch the caramel closely — burnt sugar makes the whole dish bitter.
- Pure coconut water is blander than coconut soda; add a little more sugar if using it.
- Bouillon powder is optional — swap for a smaller amount of salt, added toward the end if needed.
