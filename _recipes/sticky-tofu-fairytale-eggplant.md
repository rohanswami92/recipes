## Ingredients

**Main dish**
- 1 (16 oz) block firm tofu
- ¾ lb fairytale eggplant, halved (or regular eggplant, sliced)
- ~1 lb frozen prawns, thawed
- 6 tbsp brown sugar
- ¼ cup sambal oelek
- ¼ cup rice vinegar
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- Extra-virgin olive oil

**Cucumber salad**
- 1 large cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 small serrano pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 large scallions, greens thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- Diamond Crystal kosher salt

**To serve**
- Steamed white rice, 4 servings

## Method

1. Drain the tofu and press out moisture with paper towels. Slice into 1-inch cubes.
2. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss the tofu cubes with a little olive oil, spread on a lined
   baking sheet, and bake 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and firm. This can happen
   alongside frying the eggplant below.
3. Whisk together brown sugar, sambal oelek, rice vinegar, soy sauce, and miso paste. Set aside.
4. Make the cucumber salad: combine cucumber, serrano, scallion greens, rice vinegar, and a pinch
   of salt in a bowl. Mix and refrigerate.
5. Heat a cast iron skillet over medium heat with olive oil until just below smoking point. Add
   the eggplant, cook undisturbed 3–4 minutes until browned, then stir to brown the remaining
   sides, 2–3 minutes more.
6. Add the baked tofu to the pan with the eggplant. Pour the sauce over and stir to coat. Simmer,
   stirring occasionally, 7–8 minutes until the sauce thickens and turns sticky.
7. Stir in the thawed prawns for the last few minutes — just long enough for them to cook through
   and soak up the sauce.
8. Optional: broil 3–4 minutes for caramelized edges.
9. Serve over steamed rice, garnished with the cucumber salad.

## Notes

- Fairytale eggplant is recommended for its firm, meaty texture and quick cooking, but regular
  eggplant works fine as a swap.
- Baking the tofu instead of pan-frying it frees up the stovetop and is less fiddly than
  babysitting a skillet.
