## Ingredients

- 500 g sweet potatoes
- 1 level tsp cayenne pepper, plus extra for sprinkling
- 1 heaped tsp ground cumin, plus extra for sprinkling
- 1 level tsp ground cinnamon, plus extra for sprinkling
- Olive oil
- 1 onion
- 2 mixed-colour peppers
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 bunch fresh coriander (30 g)
- 2 fresh mixed-colour chillies
- 2 × 400 g tins beans (e.g. kidney, chickpea, pinto or cannellini)
- 2 × 400 g tins good-quality plum tomatoes
- Lime or lemon juice, or vinegar, to taste

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F, gas 6).
2. Peel the sweet potatoes, cut into bite-sized chunks and spread on a baking tray. Season with a
   pinch each of cayenne, cumin, cinnamon, sea salt and black pepper, drizzle with oil and toss
   to coat. Roast for 45 minutes to 1 hour, until golden and tender.
3. Meanwhile, peel and roughly chop the onion. Halve, deseed and roughly chop the peppers. Peel
   and finely slice the garlic. Pick the coriander leaves and finely chop the stalks. Deseed and
   finely chop the chillies.
4. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add the onion, peppers and garlic and
   cook for 5 minutes, stirring regularly.
5. Add the coriander stalks, chillies and the spices. Cook for another 5–10 minutes, stirring
   occasionally, until softened and starting to caramelise.
6. Add the beans with their liquid, then tip in the tomatoes, breaking them up with the back of a
   spoon. Stir well.
7. Bring to the boil, then reduce to medium-low and simmer for 25–30 minutes, until thickened and
   reduced. Add a splash of water if it needs loosening.
8. Stir the roasted sweet potato and most of the coriander leaves through the chilli. Taste and
   adjust the seasoning.
9. Finish with lime or lemon juice, or a splash of vinegar, to taste, and scatter over the rest of
   the coriander. Serve with yoghurt or soured cream, guacamole, and rice or tortilla chips.

## Notes

- A fresh tomato salsa or a sprinkle of chopped nuts makes a good finish.
- **Swaps:** butternut squash or regular potatoes instead of sweet potato; a leek or a few spring
  onions instead of the onion; paprika instead of cayenne; basil instead of coriander (or skip the
  herbs).
- **Freezing:** make a double or triple batch and portion it up. Cool quickly and freeze within
  2 hours. Defrost in the fridge, eat within 48 hours, and don't refreeze once reheated.
